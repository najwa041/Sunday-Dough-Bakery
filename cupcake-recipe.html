Vanilla Cupcakes

Light, tender vanilla cupcakes topped with creamy vanilla buttercream, a timeless bakery favorite.

Easy difficulty 35 Min 12 Cupcakes

Ingredients

Ingredient Guantity
All-purpose flour 180 g
Granulated Sugar 150 g
Baking powder 1½ tsp
Salt ¼ tsp
Unsalted butter, softened 115 g
Eggs 2 large
Milk 120 ml
Vanilla Extract 2 tsbp
Powdered sugar, for frosting 200 g
Unsalted butter, for frosting 100 g
Milk, for frosting 1 to 2 tbsp

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and line a 12-cup muffin tin with cupcake liners.
  2. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Beat the softened butter and granulated sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  5. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  6. Add the dry ingredients alternately with the milk, mixing just until combined.
  7. Divide the batter evenly among the cupcake liners.
  8. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  9. Cool the cupcakes completely.
  10. Beat the frosting butter until creamy, then gradually add the powdered sugar, vanilla if desired, and enough milk to achieve a pipeable consistency.
  11. Pipe or spread the frosting onto the cooled cupcakes.

Baking Tips

  • Don't overmix the batter, or the cupcakes may become dense.
  • Let the cupcakes cool completely before frosting to prevent the buttercream from melting.
  • Use a piping bag to create decorative frosting swirls.
