BAKING FAQ

Find answers to common baking questions and learn how to make your baking better.

1. Why are my cookies too hard?

Your cookies may be too hard because you baked them for too long. Try reducing the baking time.

2. Why doesn't my cake rise?

Your cake may not rise because your baking powder is old or you used the wrong measurements.

3. How do I know when my cake is ready?

Insert a toothpick into the center of the cake. If it comes out clean, your cake is ready.

4. Can I replace butter with oil?

Yes, you can replace butter with oil in some recipes. However, the texture and taste may be different.

5. Why are my muffins dry?

Your muffins may be dry because you mixed the batter too much or baked them for too long.

6. How should I measure flour?

Use a measuring cup or kitchen scale to measure flour accurately.
