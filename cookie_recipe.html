Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft, chewy, and full of chocolate chips!

Easy difficulty 25 Min 12 Cookies

Ingredients

Ingredient Guantity
All-purpose flour 190 g
Butter, softened 115 g
Brown sugar 130 g
Egg 1 large
Chocolate chips 200 g

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugars together.
  3. Mix in the egg until smooth.
  4. Add the flour and baking soda.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until golden.

Baking Tips

Chill the dough before baking for thicker cookies! Let the cookies cool before serving.
